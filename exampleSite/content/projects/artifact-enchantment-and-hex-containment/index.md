This project centers on containment methods for volatile, high-risk enchanted artifacts. It examines the stability of cursed and hex-bound relics, looking for safer ways to transport, store and neutralize them.

New containment spells and barrier enchantments developed here have contributed to improved standards in relic handling across arcane institutions. The research also shows how the enchantments on dark relics interact with external magical energies, influencing their stability and curse potency.
