This project focuses on the summoning, control and containment of interdimensional entities — a challenging area of the dark arts that involves conjuring beings from other realms. It addresses the ethical concerns surrounding these practices, as well as the dangers of unstable conjurations.

Through fieldwork and experimental summoning, we developed protocols for safer conjuration environments and containment fields that protect practitioners and their surroundings. The research also examines beneficial uses of conjured entities in protective and restorative roles, challenging the view of such beings as purely malevolent.
