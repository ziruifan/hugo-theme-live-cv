This project seeks to unravel the intricate workings of high-level curses, exploring both their structure and their ethical applications. It dives into the mechanics of advanced hexes and investigates how they can be modified for purposes beyond their traditional use, such as protection or healing.

The work involves hands-on experimentation with ancient curse artifacts, careful dissection of curse transference patterns, and the development of counter-curse methodologies. By analyzing these curses in a controlled setting, we aim to build safe frameworks for the practical deployment of dark magic under ethical guidelines.

![Calm water under a low sun](field-site.jpg "Figure 1. The tidal flats near Mysthaven, used as a neutral ground for controlled rites.")

Next, we plan to test the restorative variants of three binding curses with volunteer practitioners, and to publish the full protocol so that other institutes can reproduce the results.
