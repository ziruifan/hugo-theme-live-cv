Mysthaven's protective wards were laid down three centuries ago and have never been fully mapped. Over two seasons of fieldwork we measured ward strength along the coast and found several gaps that line up with recent, unexplained hex incidents.

The resulting atlas is now used by the city's warding office to prioritize repairs.
